How to interpret recovery

If both effort-end HR and HR after exactly 60 seconds are entered, the app calculates the difference. This is only comparable when the effort and recovery protocol are similar. A normal session graph cannot reliably identify every hockey shift or exact between-shift recovery without timestamps/markers. HR is affected by heat, stress, hydration, illness, sensor fit and other factors.

A SIMPLE ROUTINE

Hydration reminders

Use these in-app prompts as cues to check in—not as a strict fluid target.

  • School: bring a bottle and drink with regular breaks.
  • Before ice: arrive hydrated rather than trying to catch up at the last minute.
  • During: drink during breaks when possible.
  • After: drink with your recovery meal or snack.

This app cannot send reliable notifications while closed. You can use your device's Reminders app for scheduled prompts.
